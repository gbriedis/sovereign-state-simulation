# Project Vision

## Core fantasy

The player rules a small sovereign nation and shapes its development through
political decisions, institutions, infrastructure, and long-term strategy.

The nation is a living system, not a city-builder in which every person, company,
or building obeys the player directly.

## The player's role

The player holds dominant political influence but not absolute control over
society. The ruler can set policy, direct state institutions, allocate public
resources, and influence national priorities.

Outcomes emerge from physical conditions and from the reactions of citizens,
companies, institutions, and other major actors.

## An autonomous nation

Citizens, companies, institutions, and major non-player actors have their own
interests, capabilities, and constraints. They respond to the ruler and to one
another rather than acting as extensions of the player's cursor.

Economic and social responses should be plausible, including when they are
delayed, imperfect, or opposed to the ruler's intentions.

## Sovereign direction and domestic power

The player is the final strategic decision-maker, but does not originate all
national activity. Major domestic actors can identify opportunities, launch
initiatives, accumulate economic or institutional power, and pursue strategies
without first receiving a player command.

A timber entrepreneur, for example, might invest independently, lobby for roads
or rail, build an industrial cluster, choose a different region, relocate when
policy becomes hostile, or grow important enough to influence national choices.

The ruler can support, constrain, redirect, negotiate with, regulate,
nationalize, or oppose such actors. Governing is partly the exercise of sovereign
authority and partly the management of powerful interests that have agency of
their own.

## The nation is the progression screen

Progress is visible in the country itself rather than in a ruler level or generic
upgrade tree. The emotional reward comes from looking back across simulated
decades and seeing measurable national transformation: population, production,
exports, energy security, institutional performance, infrastructure, knowledge,
and living conditions.

For example, a country might move from exporting logs to engineered timber,
machinery, and software; reduce energy-import dependence; and expand a small rail
network into a national system. Those outcomes are the progression.

## Long-term product direction

The eventual game is a persistent sovereign-state MMO in which every player
nation remains deeply playable as its own simulation. Multiplayer connects
sovereign nations without weakening the internal depth, autonomy, or physical
grounding of each country.

Web3 may later provide an optional economic or ownership layer. It is not the
foundation of the simulation and is not required for the current phase.
