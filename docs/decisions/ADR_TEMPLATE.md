# ADR-NNNN: Decision title

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decision owner:** Role with authority to accept or reject
- **DRI:** One agent or role driving the decision
- **Scope:** Affected domain, contract, or paths
- **Art / Technical Art impact:** Action required / Consulted / Informed, or
  `N/A — <specific rationale>`
- **Consulted:** Required domain owners or `None`
- **Informed:** Downstream consumers or `None`
- **Supersedes:** ADR link or `None`
- **Related work:** Workstream ID(s) or `None`

## Context

What current facts, constraints, and problem make a decision necessary? Link
canonical evidence and distinguish implemented state from proposals.

## Decision

State the chosen direction precisely enough that downstream owners can act.
Include boundaries and invariants; do not hide unresolved choices inside the
decision.

## Team impact and routing

| Team or lane | Route | Required action or consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Example owner | Action required / Consulted / Informed | Concrete response |

## Alternatives considered

### Alternative name

Describe the viable alternative and why it was not selected. Preserve material
objections or conditions that could justify reconsideration.

## Consequences

### Enables

- Positive consequence.

### Costs and risks

- Tradeoff, migration cost, or failure mode.

## Adoption and verification

List the canonical documents, work packets, tests, migrations, or operational
checks required to make the accepted decision real. Acceptance is not proof of
implementation.

## Reversal or supersession

State how to reverse this safely, what evidence would trigger reconsideration,
and which later ADR supersedes it when applicable.
